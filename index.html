img2VTFImage & GIF → Team Fortress 2 spray converter

Turn any image or animated GIF into a TF2 spray (.vtf). Everything runs in your browser, so your files are never uploaded.

  1. Drop in one or more images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP and more).
  2. Drag the image to position it, and use its corner handles to resize it.
  3. Click Export VTF.
  4. In TF2, open Options → Multiplayer → Import Spray and choose the .vtf file.

TF2 sprays are limited to 512 KB; img2VTF shrinks files to fit automatically. Animated GIFs use up to 5 frames.
